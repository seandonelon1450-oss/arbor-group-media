## The six kinds of math underneath

**1. Fluid dynamics — the original science.**
Pressure, velocity, turbulence, smooth (laminar) flow. G.A.U.G.E.™ thinks this way about a business: customers, calls, and money are flow. A missed call is turbulence. A booked job is smooth flow.

**2. Network flow — moving the most, without a bottleneck.**
Computing's flow algorithms answer one question: how do you move the maximum through a network before something chokes? That is exactly a business day — calls in, jobs out, money through — and it is the closed loop, stated as math.

**3. Queueing — how lines form and drain.**
The math of waiting. One unanswered phone builds pressure that costs more than the call itself. Answer instantly and the pressure never builds. This is why Missed-Call Rescue™ exists.

**4. Feedback control — the closed loop is a discipline, not a slogan.**
Sense what is happening, correct it, hold the flow steady. Thermostats and cruise control run on it. So do VESTA™ and KIARA™: answer, book, confirm, follow up — the loop senses itself and closes.

**5. Search — authority flows, and friction is real.**
Google's PageRank is a flow algorithm: authority moves through links the way fluid moves through channels, and its damping factor works as **friction** in that flow. Sean's propulsion-engineering bachelor's work was on exactly this — how friction ratios affect how search engines work. The marriage of fluid dynamics and information was never a metaphor. It was the thesis.

**6. Modern AI — the newest models run on flow.**
Today's generative AI is built on flow mathematics: noise flowed, step by step, into structure. Agent systems route information the same way the Arbor model describes — pressure here, open a channel there. The industry arrived, years later, at the math Sean started from.
